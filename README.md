# RETAIL_SALES

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using SQL, Python, and Power BI to uncover business insights, identify profit drivers, evaluate product performance, and provide actionable recommendations for improving sales and profitability.

## Objectives

* Analyze sales and profit performance across categories, products, customers, and regions.
* Identify high-performing and loss-making products.
* Understand the impact of discounts on profitability.
* Discover customer and regional sales trends.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.

## Key Business Insights

### Insight 1

Technology category generated the highest profit among all categories.

### Insight 2

Several products generated high sales but low profit.

### Insight 3

Regional performance varied significantly across the business.

### Insight 4

Higher discounts were associated with lower profitability.

### Insight 5

A small number of customers contributed a large percentage of revenue.

### Insight 6

Some sub-categories consistently generated losses.

### Insight 7

Sales showed seasonal trends across months.

### Insight 8

Profit margins differed significantly across categories.

### Insight 9

Top products contributed disproportionately to total revenue.

### Insight 10

High sales did not always translate into high profit.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Reduce excessive discounting.
* Focus marketing efforts on high-margin products.
* Improve performance in low-profit regions.
* Create loyalty programs for high-value customers.
* Review pricing strategies for low-profit products.
* Consider discontinuing consistently loss-making products.
